3. Synthesized Answer with Sources

What's the difference between the 5-year and 2-year Master in Logistics?

Great question! The main difference is the starting point:

  • The 5-year integrated Master's is for students starting directly after high school. It combines a Bachelor's and Master's degree into one seamless program.
  • The 2-year Master's is for students who already hold a relevant Bachelor's degree.

Both lead to the same Master of Science degree and specialization opportunities.
