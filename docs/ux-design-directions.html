Design Direction Showcase

These mockups explore different visual and interaction styles for the chatbot interface, using the "Mountain Majesty" theme. Focus on the overall layout, density, and feel of the conversation. Which direction feels most aligned with the goal of an "effortless and precise" experience?

1. Classic Bubbles

Familiar, friendly, and intuitive. Uses traditional rounded bubbles for a classic chat feel.

Hi there! How can I help you find information about studies at Høgskolen i Molde?

Tell me about combining logistics and economics.

2. Sharp & Modern

Clean and contemporary. Uses squared corners and outlined bot messages for a modern, structured look.

3. Spacious Minimalist

Airy and focused. Uses extra white space and subtle shadows to create a calm, premium feel.

4. Card-Based Bot

Structured and clear. Bot responses are formatted as distinct cards, separating content from sources.
